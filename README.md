### 🚀 Currently in Development!  
Some features may be incomplete or subject to change. Follow for updates!

### 🎵 What is FlowMode?

**FlowMode** is a web app that helps you stay productive by integrating a Pomodoro-style timer with your favorite Spotify playlists.  
Alternate between work ("Flow") and break ("Rest") periods while automatically playing curated music to match your focus cycles.

### ✨ Features

- ⏳ **Pomodoro Timer** – Set work and rest intervals to enhance productivity.
- 🎶 **Spotify Integration** – Play different playlists during work and rest sessions.
- 🔀 **Shuffle & Playback Controls** – Easily toggle shuffle and playback.
- 📊 **Cycle Tracking** – Monitor remaining cycles for better time management.
